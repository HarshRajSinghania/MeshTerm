Vendored by packaging/notices.py -- not part of any pyobjc distribution.

Two pyobjc wheels on PyPI ship no license file of any kind: pyobjc-core 12.2.2 and
pyobjc-framework-libdispatch 12.2.2 carry an MIT license classifier and nothing matching
LICENSE*, LICENCE*, COPYING*, or NOTICE* in their dist-info -- verified by inspecting the
macOS universal2 wheels directly (the two sibling wheels bleak also pulls in,
pyobjc-framework-Cocoa and pyobjc-framework-CoreBluetooth, do carry licenses/LICENSE.txt
and are read from there). First caught by the macOS legs of the 0.3.3 release build, which
refused to bundle them unnoticed; they only appear in a macOS build's closure, since bleak
marks them sys_platform == "darwin".

All pyobjc packages are built and published together from the one pyobjc repository and
share its single license text, reproduced below unmodified. Pulled 2026-09-13 from:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ronaldoussoren/pyobjc/master/pyobjc-core/License.txt
Its copyright range (2003-2025) covers the pinned 12.2.2 release.
